# Team Member Photos

Place team member photos here with the following naming convention:

## Required Photos:
- `yassir-edraoui.jpg` - Yassir Edraoui (CEO)
- `sohaib-elabidi.jpg` - Sohaib El Abidi (COO & CFO)
- `soukaina-essaidi.jpg` - Soukaina Essaidi (Technical Lead)
- `adam-rahda.jpg` - Adam Rahda (Head of Business Development)

## Image Specifications:
- **Format:** JPG or PNG
- **Dimensions:** 800x800px (square, 1:1 ratio)
- **File Size:** < 500KB
- **Background:** Professional headshot with clean background
- **Quality:** High resolution, well-lit

## Recommended Settings:
- Professional business casual attire
- Neutral or blurred background
- Good lighting (natural or studio)
- Centered composition
- Smiling/approachable expression

## Free Photo Resources (if needed):
- **AI Headshot Generators:**
  - PhotoAI.io
  - HeadshotPro.com
  - ProfilePicture.AI

- **Stock Photos (temporary placeholders):**
  - Unsplash.com
  - Pexels.com
  - UI Faces (uifaces.co)

## After Adding Photos:
1. Place images in this folder: `public/team/`
2. The website will automatically display them on the Team page
3. Ensure filenames match exactly (case-sensitive)
